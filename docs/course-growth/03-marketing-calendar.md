# 12-Month Pacific Northwest Golf Revenue Calendar

Standardized skeleton, built once and customized ~20% per course (course-specific event dates,
local weather patterns east vs. west of the Cascades, and Week-1-audit priorities). 4–6 core
campaign themes per month, mapped to the four pillars: **Grow** the audience, **Get Back**
lapsed golfers, **Increase Value**, **Fill Inventory**.

Assumes a typical western-Oregon/Willamette-Valley/Portland-metro season (wet shoulder months,
playable most of the year, real winter lull Dec–Jan). For high-desert/eastern-Oregon or
Bend-area courses, shift the "season opening" and "winter lull" windows — noted inline.

---

### January — Foundation & New Year
- New Year's membership/pass sale (early-bird pricing, deadline-driven)
- Winter league registration (indoor simulator leagues where available)
- Database reactivation: "haven't seen you since [month]" to 180/365-day lapsed segment
- Gift card push (New Year's resolution gifting angle, secondary to Dec)
- Off-season F&B/event promotion (private events, banquet space)
- *(East-side/Bend courses: lean harder into simulator/indoor and pass pre-sale; course itself may be closed)*

### February — Pre-Season Momentum
- Membership/pass sale continues — mid-tier urgency deadline
- Early-bird league registration
- "Get your game ready" lessons push (ahead of season)
- Punch-card / advance tee-time bundle promotion
- Weather-opportunity campaign: any unseasonably good weekend gets a same-week "it's open, come
  play" send (standing template, always ready)

### March — Season Opening
- Season-opening announcement + course conditions update (grass, tee/green status)
- Spring aeration schedule communication (set expectations early, reduce complaint volume)
- League registration deadline push
- Membership/pass final-call (last chance before season pricing)
- Masters week tie-in (early April overlap in some years — content/engagement angle, not a discount)

### April — Spring Ramp
- Masters week campaign (viewing parties, junior clinics, content tie-in)
- Spring course-conditions update #2 (post-aeration recovery messaging if applicable)
- Junior golf program promotion (spring clinics/camps)
- Corporate outing booking push (Q2/Q3 calendar filling)
- Twilight rate re-introduction as days lengthen

### May — Peak Season Begins
- Mother's Day (gift cards, mother/family round packages, brunch/F&B tie-in)
- Memorial Day weekend (outing/tournament push, weekend availability campaign)
- Membership referral push ("bring a friend" — leverages members already in-season)
- League mid-season engagement (standings, social hook)
- Summer camp/junior golf registration

### June — Full Season
- Father's Day (gift cards, father/child round packages)
- Summer twilight campaign ramps (longest days — real inventory to fill after 4–5pm)
- Corporate outing season kicks off — dedicated outing-booking campaign
- Junior golf camps in full swing — promotion + parent-targeted upsell (lessons, range cards)
- Wedding/event space promotion if applicable (banquet, patio)

### July — Peak Demand Management
- Fourth of July (outing/tournament tie-in, F&B/patio promotion)
- Tournament season push (member tournaments, city/regional qualifiers)
- Heat/good-weather early-morning and twilight inventory campaign
- Visitor/tourist campaign (if course draws destination play — different segment, different message
  than local database)
- Gift card mid-summer push (low-key, low-frequency touch)

### August — Late Summer
- Continued tournament/outing season support
- "Beat the heat" early-tee-time and twilight double-down
- Membership renewal pre-communication begins (plant the seed 60–90 days before fall renewal cycle)
- Junior golf season wrap-up / fall clinic teaser
- Last-minute-availability campaign template goes into regular rotation (weather-driven cancellations create same-week inventory)

### September — Fall Transition
- Fall golf campaign ("best conditions of the year, fewer crowds")
- Membership renewal push begins in earnest (renewal deadline messaging)
- Corporate outing push for fall (many companies run fall outings)
- League fall/winter session registration
- Aerification communication if scheduled in this window (set expectations, offer discounted or
  alternate-course play if applicable)

### October — Shoulder Season
- Fall golf continues — "playable and beautiful" push, leverages lighter fall crowds
- Aerification communication if scheduled here instead of September
- Halloween-themed light touch (fun F&B/event promotion, low priority)
- Gift card campaign begins ramping toward holiday season
- Weather-opportunity campaign becomes more frequent as conditions get more variable

### November — Holiday Ramp
- Black Friday / Small Business Saturday gift card and pass pre-sale (highest-leverage gift card
  window of the year)
- Membership/pass early-bird for next season launches here (captures cash flow before year-end)
- Thanksgiving week F&B/event promotion
- Reactivation campaign: golfers who played in spring/summer but haven't been back — last push
  before winter lull
- Corporate holiday party / banquet space promotion

### December — Gift & Renewal Season
- Christmas gift card push (the single highest-volume gift card period of the year — dedicated
  multi-touch campaign, not a single email)
- New Year's membership/pass sale teaser (build the list of interested members before the January
  campaign launches)
- Year-end "thank you" / season recap send (relationship touch, not a hard sell)
- Holiday golf availability (many golfers have time off — an underused inventory opportunity)
- Off-season restaurant/private event promotion (holiday parties, banquets)

---

## Notes for course-specific customization (the 20%)

- Insert the course's actual member-guest, club championship, and city/regional tournament dates.
- Insert actual aerification windows once known (courses often shift them year to year based on
  agronomy).
- East-side/high-desert and mountain-adjacent courses: compress the season-opening/closing
  windows and lean harder into indoor/simulator and pass pre-sale during the true winter closure.
- Resort/destination courses: add a "visitor/tourist" track running in parallel with the local
  database track in the peak months, since messaging (rack rate value vs. local loyalty) differs.
- Always keep a standing "weather opportunity" template ready year-round — PNW weather creates
  same-week fill-the-tee-sheet opportunities in every month, not just shoulder season.
