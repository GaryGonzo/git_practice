# OGG Course Growth — Positioning, Offer & Pricing

Business unit of **Oregon Golf Guide**. This document is the foundation everything else in
`docs/course-growth/` and `packages/course-growth-site/` is built from. Read this one first.

---

## 1. Brand architecture

| Side | Name | Audience | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer | **Discover Oregon Golf** | Golfers | Course discovery, guides, rankings, content, social, (future) golfer email/SMS audience |
| B2B | **OGG Course Growth** | Course GMs/owners/Directors of Golf | Done-for-you lifecycle marketing & revenue growth |
| Parent | **Oregon Golf Guide** | Both | Umbrella brand, credibility, long-term connective tissue |

Tagline for the parent brand across both sides: **"Helping Oregon's golf courses grow."**
Long-term thesis: the consumer audience (Discover Oregon Golf) eventually becomes a distribution
channel course clients can tap — real golfers, not stock traffic. Do not build or promise that
channel yet; it is a year-2+ upsell, not part of the initial $2,000/mo offer. Mentioning it once
on the About page as "in progress" is fine; do not sell it.

## 2. Positioning (final)

**We are not a marketing agency. We are a revenue function for golf courses.**

Core positioning statement (used everywhere — website, sales calls, contracts):

> **We help golf courses make more money from the golfers and website traffic they already have.**

Supporting line used in outreach and on sales calls (this is the wedge — it reframes the
conversation away from "do you need marketing help" and toward "you already did the hard part"):

> **You've already spent years earning these golfers. We help you generate more revenue from them.**

What this means in practice:
- We do not talk about "brand awareness," "engagement," or "impressions."
- We do not open with "digital marketing." We open with tee sheet, membership, and F&B revenue.
- Every deliverable ties to a specific revenue stream: tee times, memberships/passes, leagues,
  tournaments/outings, lessons, range, gift cards, merchandise, F&B, events, repeat visits,
  reactivation.
- We talk like people who understand a tee sheet, a shoulder season, and aerification — not like
  people who talk about "synergy" or "digital transformation."

### Words we never use
Full-service agency, 360-degree solutions, synergy, disruptive, cutting-edge, growth hacking,
digital transformation, "would you like help with your marketing," best-in-class, holistic.

## 3. Final offer

### Name
**OGG Course Growth** (formal: *Oregon Golf Guide – Course Growth*)

### One-line offer
> A done-for-you revenue system for your tee sheet, memberships, and F&B — built in 6 weeks,
> run every month, guaranteed to pay for itself.

### What we sell
1. A **6-Week Course Revenue Buildout** — audit, list-growth engine, segmentation, automated
   revenue journeys, a 12-month campaign calendar, and a live reporting dashboard.
2. **Ongoing monthly management** — we run the calendar, the automations, and the reporting,
   every month, for as long as the course stays a client.

### Chosen headline territory

Three headline directions were tested against the positioning. Decision:

| Headline | Use |
|---|---|
| **"Make $2 For Every $1 You Spend With Us — Guaranteed."** | **Primary hero headline** (website, top of proposal). Strongest, most specific, most differentiated. Always paired with a `*` and a one-line eligibility qualifier directly beneath it, and a link to full guarantee terms. |
| "Your Golfers Are Already There. We Help You Earn More From Them." | **Primary line for cold outreach and sales calls.** Leading with a guarantee in a cold email reads as gimmicky before trust exists; leading with "you already did the hard part" earns the next sentence. Used as the subhead everywhere the $2:$1 headline appears. |
| "More Rounds. More Members. More Revenue." | Secondary/supporting line — section headers, ad copy, signage, one-pagers. Not the lead anywhere. |

Rationale: the $2:$1 guarantee is our single strongest point of differentiation — almost no
competitor in this space will put a number on it — so it earns the hero slot on the site, where a
visitor already arrived with some intent and can scroll to see the qualification. In cold outreach,
where trust is at zero, leading with "you already earned these golfers" outperforms leading with a
number that can read as a sales trick before any credibility is established.

## 4. Guarantee (marketing-copy version)

> **We guarantee at least $2 in trackable, incremental revenue for every $1 you pay us over a
> 90-day measurement window — or you don't pay your management fee again until we hit it.**

This is a **90-day rolling threshold**, not a month-by-month requirement. Golf revenue is lumpy
(weather, shoulder season, one big outing skews a month) — a 90-day window is the shortest period
that's fair to both sides. Full plain-English terms, eligibility requirements, and
attorney-review flags are in `08-guarantee-legal.md`. **Marketing copy on the website and in sales
conversations must always route to that document's plain-English terms before a contract is
signed — never let the guarantee be verbally over-promised beyond what's written there.**

## 5. Pricing structure

Kept deliberately simple — one core offer, not six tiers.

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **6-Week Course Revenue Buildout** | **$1,500 one-time setup fee** | Due at signing. Covers the audit, list-growth build, segmentation, automation build, calendar, and dashboard setup. Below effort cost early on — treat it as a qualification filter and pipeline-fill tool, not a profit center. Waived in the 90-Day Pilot offer (see below) to remove first-dollar friction with founding clients. |
| **Ongoing Management** | **$2,000/month** | Flat. Month-to-month after the initial 90-day term. No usage tiers, no per-email pricing. |
| **Initial term** | **90 days minimum** | Aligns with the guarantee's measurement window — a course can't fairly judge (or we fairly prove) revenue impact faster than that. After 90 days: month-to-month, cancel with 30 days' notice. |
| **Annual prepay discount** | **10% off** ($21,600/yr instead of $24,000) | Optional, offered only after month 4+ once trust is established. Never pitched on the first call. |
| **Additional location** | **$1,500/month per additional course** | Same ownership group, same POS/ESP where possible. Setup fee reduced to $750 for a second location on an existing account (most of the audit/brand work carries over). |
| **Paid media management add-on** | **+$500/month + ad spend** | Google/Meta ads to the golfer database's lookalikes and website retargeting. Not included in base scope. Offered starting month 3+ once organic/owned channels are proven. |
| **Website conversion add-on** | **$750 one-time** | Landing-page/booking-flow conversion work beyond popup/signup install. Only sold if the audit surfaces a real leak (e.g., broken booking funnel). |
| **Photography/content add-on** | **Referral only, not resold** | We refer a local photographer/videographer partner. We do not mark it up or manage it — stated explicitly in the SOW as out of scope (see `02-monthly-service-and-reporting.md`). |

No setup-fee-waiver, no "starter/growth/pro" tiers, no per-seat pricing. One service, one price,
a handful of clearly-scoped add-ons sold only after the core relationship is proven.

**Why $2,000/month is right for this market:** independent/municipal/semi-private Oregon courses
rarely have an internal marketing hire (that role runs $50–70k/yr loaded). $2,000/mo
($24k/yr) is a fraction of that cost, is an easy budget-line approval for a GM (not board-level),
and only requires ~$4,000–6,000 in verifiable incremental monthly revenue to already be paying for
itself well past the 2:1 bar — which for a course doing even modest volume is very achievable
through better use of existing traffic and existing database, before any new spend.

## 6. Scope of Work — summary

Full detail in `02-monthly-service-and-reporting.md`. Headline structure:

**In scope, every month:**
- 4–6 email campaigns, 2–4 SMS campaigns (when SMS is enabled/appropriate)
- Monthly promotional calendar (from the standardized 12-month calendar, customized ~20%)
- Automated flow management (the 12 standard revenue journeys)
- Database growth management (popup/signup, QR capture points, list hygiene)
- Segmentation maintenance
- Reactivation campaigns (60/90/180/365-day lapsed golfer flows)
- Tee-time inventory campaigns (slow weekdays, twilight, shoulder season, last-minute)
- Membership/pass marketing
- Tournament/outing/league marketing support
- Revenue attribution & monthly reporting
- Quarterly strategy review call

**Explicitly out of scope** (sold separately or not offered):
- Daily/organic social media management
- Photography or video production
- Full website redesign or rebuild
- Golfer-facing customer support
- From-scratch graphic design on every single campaign (we use a templated content library —
  see `06-operations-and-sops.md`)
- Unlimited revision rounds (2 rounds per asset, defined in the QA/approval SOP)
- Constant/ad hoc meetings (one working touchpoint per month + quarterly review, not on-demand)
- Paid media management (add-on only)

## 7. Four pillars (frame for the monthly service)

1. **Grow The Audience** — grow the course's owned email/SMS database.
2. **Get Golfers Back** — increase repeat rounds, reactivate lapsed golfers.
3. **Increase Customer Value** — memberships, passes, tournaments, leagues, F&B, lessons,
   merchandise, gift cards.
4. **Fill Perishable Inventory** — sell tee times that would otherwise expire unused (slow
   weekdays, twilight, open weekend inventory, good-weather opportunities, last-minute
   availability, post-aerification, shoulder season).

Every campaign, automation, and report ties back to one of these four pillars. This is the
internal operating frame *and* the language used on the "What's Included" page.

## 8. The 80/20 standardization principle

This is the single most important operating constraint for solo-to-10-clients capacity:

- **~80% standardized across every client:** the 6-week buildout process, the 12 automation
  flows (logic/timing/structure), the 12-month calendar skeleton, segmentation framework,
  reporting dashboard format, QA/approval process, campaign build workflow, contract, guarantee
  terms, onboarding checklist.
- **~20% customized per client:** brand voice/visuals, specific offers and pricing, course-specific
  events (member-guest, city tournament, etc.), local seasonality quirks, POS/ESP-specific
  integration details, course-specific segment thresholds (e.g., what counts as a "frequent
  golfer" varies by course size).

Every SOP and template in this project is written to make that 80% ratio real, not aspirational.
