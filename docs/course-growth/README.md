## Quick facts

- **Offer:** 6-Week Course Revenue Buildout ($1,500) + $2,000/month ongoing management
- **Guarantee:** $2 in trackable revenue per $1 paid, measured over a rolling 90-day window, or
  the management fee is waived until it's met (details + eligibility in doc 08)
- **Target:** independent public/municipal/semi-private Oregon courses and small resorts
- **Solo capacity target:** ~10 clients; first hire triggers around client #9–10 (doc 07)
